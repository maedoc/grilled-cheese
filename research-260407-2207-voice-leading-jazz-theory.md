## Key Findings

- Guide tones (3rd and 7th) are the foundation of jazz voice leading
- ii-V-I voice leading: 7th of each chord resolves down a half-step to 3rd of next chord; 3rd sustains as 7th of next
- Chords related by 5th produce contrary motion in guide tones; by 2nd produce parallel motion
- Complete chord-scale mapping: Maj7→Ionian/Lydian, Dom7→Mixolydian/LydDom/Altered/HWDim, Min7→Dorian/Aeolian/Phrygian, Half-dim→Locrian/Locrian#2, Dim7→Octatonic
- Avoid notes: Maj7 avoids 4th, Dom7 avoids 4th (in major context), min7 has none (Dorian)
- Functional context determines available tensions on V7: resolving to I (major) allows natural or altered; resolving to i (minor) typically uses b9/b13
- Chord voicing generation depends on: chord quality, function, melody note, and voice leading context
- Voice leading matrices visualize multiple voicing paths through progressions with smooth voice movement
- The Fluid Voicing Generator (ICMC 2016) provides an algorithmic approach using weighted probability
